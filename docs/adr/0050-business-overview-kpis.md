# ADR 0050: Business overview KPIs in the owner panel

Status: accepted — point 3 decided by the owner on 2026-10-14: keep list-price MRR (before discounts) as implemented; the other points are still to be confirmed with ADR 0045–0054. Owner's backlog item 2026-10-13: «Обзор» (MRR and KPI). Definitions follow spec/panels/OWNER-PANEL.md §3; deviations are listed here.

## Decisions

1. **Scope**: `GET /platform-api/v1/overview` (`billing.read`: owner, billing), mode LIVE (default) or TEST, whole UTC days (default the last 30 days including today, max 366), previous period of equal length for money and churn. Currencies are never summed together, no FX; LIVE and TEST never mixed.
2. **Customers now**: paying = organisations with an active provider contract whose paid period has not ended (trials, free overrides and pending checkouts excluded); trial = running internal trials; past due = contracts past_due/unpaid; cancel scheduled = paying contracts set to cancel at period end.
3. **Contracted MRR — deviation**: the spec says "after recurring discount". We do not store the discount actually applied to each contract's recurring price (only grants and a per-period ledger used for stacking), so MRR is the **list** recurring price (base + extra stores × their price + faster checks, from the contract's price version), without tax and **before discounts**; a yearly price counts 1/12 per month. The card shows how many contracts have an active discount grant ("not subtracted") and contracts without a catalog price ("not counted"). Exact fraction `numerator_minor/denominator`, the panel rounds half up only for display and marks it "≈". **Owner decision 2026-10-14: keep list-price MRR as it is.**
4. **Money in the period**: collected = transactions `paid`/`completed` by `billed_at`, total incl. tax (tax shown separately); refunds = refunds in status `refunded` by the provider confirmation time; net = collected − refunds (may be negative). Not payouts and not profit.
5. **Paid churn**: paid contracts cancelled whose paid period ended inside the period ÷ paid (non-trial) contracts created before the period start that were active or still paid at it; a scheduled cancellation is not churn yet. Shown with both counts.
6. **Freshness**: `last_synced_at` = newest provider update of contracts/transactions in that mode; none → "data incomplete, not zero revenue".
7. **Not yet**: trial conversion (the cohort window must be approved), drill-down to the exact list of documents behind each number (cards link to the subscriptions/payments lists with filters), time-series charts, cost/margin.
