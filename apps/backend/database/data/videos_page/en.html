Three short videos recorded on a test shop with the real service: what Business Watchdog checks, what it finds and how it tells you.

The checkout check

A browser walks your shop like a customer — product, cart, checkout — and checks that the payment form really appears. It never places an order or enters card data.

When customers cannot pay

The Stripe key of the shop stopped working: customers see an error instead of the card form. The check notices it, names the cause and alerts you.

Marked as paid, but no money

A card was declined, yet the order ended up as paid. Business Watchdog compares the order with Stripe and reports the €18.40 gap.

How these videos were made

They were recorded on a test shop with Stripe in test mode. The checks, incidents and e-mails you see were produced by the service itself; the shop name and customer are made up.
