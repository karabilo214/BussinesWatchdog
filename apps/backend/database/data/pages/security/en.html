Six promises behind every connection

They are built into the service, not just written down: where a platform allows it, the service refuses access that is wider than it needs.

01

Read-only by design

A WooCommerce key with write permission and a full Stripe secret key are refused when you connect them.

02

No customer data needed

No names, addresses, e-mails, phone numbers or order items of your customers are requested.

03

Keys, not passwords

You never share an admin password. Keys are stored encrypted; after saving, only their last characters are visible.

Stored in Germany

Servers and encrypted backups are in Germany. We process your store data as a processor under Art. 28 GDPR.

05

Never buys anything

The checkout check stops at the payment form. It never places an order and never enters card details.

You stay in control

WooCommerce: a key that can only read

You create the key yourself in WooCommerce and choose the permission. Business Watchdog checks it when you connect.

  • Only a key with the permission Read is accepted. The service checks the key without writing anything and refuses a key that could write.
  • Only reading requests (GET) to a fixed list of addresses. Any other request is blocked before it leaves the service.
  • Only the order fields listed here are requested. Billing and shipping addresses, the customer and the order items are not.
  • The order key is stored only as a one-way hash.

The only requests sent to your store

GET /wp-json/wc/v3
GET /wp-json/wc/v3/orders
GET /wp-json/wc/v3/orders/{id}/refunds
GET /wp-json/wc/v3/webhooks

Order fields requested (_fields)

id, number, status, currency, total,
payment_method, payment_method_title,
transaction_id, order_key, created_via,
date_created_gmt, date_modified_gmt,
date_paid_gmt, refunds, meta_data

WooCommerce cannot return single meta keys, so from meta_data the service keeps only payment references (payment mode, the provider’s refund ID) and discards everything else immediately.

Stripe: only a restricted key with read rights

Stripe lets you create a restricted key that can do nothing but read. That is the only kind of key Business Watchdog accepts.

  • A full secret key (sk_…) is refused with the message: «Only a restricted read-only key (rk_…) is accepted».
  • Only reading requests: payments, charges, refunds and — if you allow it — disputes and fees.
  • Permissions you do not grant simply stay unavailable; the dashboard tells you what is not visible instead of guessing.

PayPal: where read-only does not exist, we say so

PayPal cannot issue read-only credentials

By PayPal’s rules, even an app with only Transaction Search may also refund payments. Business Watchdog never does that: it calls only reading methods from a fixed list and blocks every other request before it is sent. The dashboard shows this warning before you connect, and you confirm it.

The only PayPal methods the service calls

POST /v1/oauth2/token          sign in
GET  /v1/reporting/transactions
GET  /v2/checkout/orders/{id}
GET  /v2/payments/captures/{id}
GET  /v2/payments/refunds/{id}
GET  /v2/payments/authorizations/{id}
GET  /v1/customer/disputes      optional
GET  /v1/customer/disputes/{id} optional
GET  /v1/notifications/webhooks
POST /v1/notifications/verify-webhook-signature

The two POST requests only sign in and check that a webhook really came from PayPal. Credentials are stored encrypted, and you can delete the app in PayPal at any time.

Shopware: permissions you see before you install

Before installing the Business Watchdog App, Shopware shows you its permissions — they are all read permissions. With an integration key, you create a role that may only view orders.

Permissions of the App (manifest.xml)

<permissions>
    <read>order</read>
    <read>order_transaction</read>
    <read>order_transaction_capture</read>
    <read>order_transaction_capture_refund</read>
    <read>currency</read>
    <read>payment_method</read>
    <read>state_machine</read>
    <read>state_machine_state</read>
</permissions>
  • The fields of each order are limited explicitly: number, amount, currency, states, payment method and times — no customers, addresses or line items.
  • Shopware searches with POST requests; the service may send only these — reads, plus the App’s own two records.
POST /api/oauth/token
POST /api/search/order

Honestly: Shopware lets even a «view only» role change order, payment and delivery states. The service never sends such requests — they are not on the list above.

The WooCommerce plugin: optional and easy to stop

The plugin is not required — a read-only key is enough. It adds instant updates and payment attempt counts. It is published in the WordPress.org plugin directory, reviewed by the WordPress.org plugin team.

It sends

  • The same order and refund facts as the key, but instantly
  • Counts of payment attempts per payment method
  • Only if you switch it on: counts of checkout steps

It never

  • Sends names, addresses, e-mails, phone numbers, order items, IP addresses or visitor IDs
  • Takes part in payment, order creation, stock or store e-mails
  • Slows your store down: if the service is unreachable, your shop keeps working

The checkout check never buys anything

A browser walks through your store like a shopper — product, cart, checkout — and stops at the payment form.

  • Every request that would place an order, pay, capture or refund is blocked.
  • If you allow the full payment form check, the scripts of Stripe, PayPal and other providers are loaded read-only; any writing request to them is blocked too.
  • Input fields and addresses are masked in the check’s screenshots.
  • The optional observation script sets no cookies, stores nothing on your customers’ devices, reads no form fields and reports only to your own store.

GDPR questions

Is Business Watchdog a processor under the GDPR?

Yes. We process your stores’ data on your behalf as a processor under Art. 28 GDPR. The data processing terms are part of our Terms of Service (Section 15).

Where is the data stored?

On servers in Germany, with daily encrypted backups that are also kept in Germany. Details, including where the service is operated from, are in the Privacy Policy.

Which other services receive data?

Contabo GmbH runs the servers and backups. Paddle handles subscription payments as an independent controller. Telegram receives alert texts only if you connect a Telegram chat.

Do my customers have to consent to checkout observation?

The observation script sets no cookies, stores nothing on the device and collects no personal data of your customers. In the dashboard you find a ready paragraph for your privacy policy and a legitimate interest assessment template. Please review them with your own legal adviser.

How long is data kept?

Order history for 90, 180 or 365 days depending on the plan, check screenshots for 30 days, account data until 30 days after the account is closed.

Can I have my data deleted?

Yes. Deleting your organisation removes its account data within 30 days, unless a legal retention duty applies. Revoking keys stops new data at once.
